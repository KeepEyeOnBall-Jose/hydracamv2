HydraCam Account And Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-06-15

Request Deletion

HydraCam users can request deletion of account-linked data by emailing jose@keepeyeonball.com from the email address used for HydraCam sign-in. Include the HydraCam GUID shown in the app when available.

Data Covered

  • Auth0 account identity data associated with the HydraCam login.
  • HydraCam backend user records and identifiers, including HydraCam GUID and linked device/session identifiers.
  • Uploaded session metadata, photos, videos, audio, and user-selected gallery media associated with the verified account.
  • Support diagnostics or local logs sent to the support team or backend for troubleshooting.

Data stored only on a user's device must be removed from that device by the user, either through HydraCam's local media/session controls or by uninstalling the app.

Verification

Before deleting account-linked data, HydraCam verifies that the requester controls the email address or Auth0 identity connected to the account. A HydraCam GUID may be used as an additional locator, not as sole proof of identity.

Timing

Verified deletion requests are reviewed and actioned as soon as practical. Some limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute handling, or backup recovery.
